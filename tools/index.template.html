IMPORTANT · DSC TOKEN SOFTWARE

Check your token software first.

Before running the Digital Signer Service isLive test:

  1. Connect your DSC USB token to this computer.
  2. Make sure the correct software/driver for your token is installed and running.
  3. Open the token software and confirm it detects your token and signing certificate.

After the signer test: if isLive shows success but signing still fails, recheck the token software and certificate, then retry signing.

isLive checks the signer service. It does not confirm that your DSC token is connected or ready. This website cannot inspect your token software.
